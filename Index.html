OUTLAW: LAST FRONTIER

A first playable 3D browser prototype for the original survival-crime RPG. Explore a frontier, gather materials, grow your stats, take risks for cash, build a camp, travel, and form a gang.

How to play: use the directional buttons to move. Tap the action buttons to interact. This is an early prototype; progress resets when you reload.
